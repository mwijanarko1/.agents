# Claim and asset honesty

Hard rules for any marketing, promo, launch, social-ad, or motion B-roll video. Apply on `/social-ad-15s`, `/product-launch-video`, `/motion-broll`, and any freeform build that sells a product or visualizes claims from speech.

## Claims

- Read the destination page (or its source in the repo) before writing copy. Prices, counts, speeds, guarantees, ratings, and "free" claims must appear on that page or in user-supplied materials.
- If the user asks for copy that contradicts the page, do it, but flag the mismatch once in the delivery note.
- Prefer outcome language the page already uses. Do not invent a stronger promise than the product states.

## Assets

- Only use real assets: user-supplied files, project assets, or images captured from the live product/site.
- Never invent customers, reviews, ratings, logos of other companies, or results.
- If a proof row has no real number to show, omit proof. Do not fill with placeholder social proof.
- Never fabricate a person. If a beat needs more images of someone than exist, use crops or mirrors of real ones and say so in the delivery note.
- Missing images may render as labelled placeholders during preview. They must not ship in the final MP4 unless the user accepts them.

## Brand and tone

- Match the brand (colors, font, logo, tone), not template defaults.
- Default to no hype, no exclamation marks, no emoji unless the brand uses them on the destination page.

## B-roll and on-screen data

- Never invent numbers, quotes, prices, token counts, or results for charts, bars, terminals, or cards.
- When the transcript lacks figures, use relative bar lengths, skeleton text, or labels taken from the spoken words.
- List every illustrative element in the delivery note (`plan.json` notes / `TIMING.md` for `/motion-broll`).

## Delivery note

Always report: file path, beat-by-beat or clip-by-clip copy, and any claim/asset caveats (low-res sources, crops, placeholders left in, copy that differs from the landing page, illustrative B-roll data).
